Privacy Policy "Get Done AI App"

Get Done AI • Last Updated: October 2026

🔒
Our Commitment to Your Privacy
We respect your privacy completely. Get Done AI - To-Do List is designed to operate locally on your device without collecting, transmitting, or selling any of your personal data to external servers.

🛡️ App Permissions Used

🔔
Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)
Used strictly to send task reminder notifications at the exact times you set. No notification data is sent to external servers.
🌐
Internet Access (INTERNET)
Used to communicate with the Gemini AI Assistant when you explicitly ask a prompt, and to serve advertisements via Google AdMob. Your personal task database is never sent automatically.
⏰
Exact Scheduling & Wake Lock (WAKE_LOCK / SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM)
Ensures your scheduled task notifications trigger reliably at exact set times, even when your device screen is off.
⚡
Boot Completed (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED)
Automatically reschedules your pending task notifications after device restarts so you never miss an important reminder.

📢 Advertising (Google AdMob)

Our application integrates Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google AdMob may collect and process non-personal technical data (such as anonymous device identifiers or general diagnostic logs) strictly for ad serving and fraud prevention in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. We do not share any of your personal tasks or database contents with advertisers.

📥 What Data We Collect

✓ Task Data: Titles, descriptions, due dates, times, and priorities you enter are stored locally on your device database.
✓ App Preferences: Language, theme mode, and notification settings stored locally on your device.
✓ Reminder Schedules: Processed entirely locally on your phone without cloud storage.

🚫 What Data We Do NOT Collect

✕ Your name, email address, phone number, or personal identity information.
✕ Your geographic GPS location.
✕ Contacts, photos, media, or device storage files.
✕ Analytics data, tracking metrics, or behavioral logs.
✕ Device IDs, advertising identifiers, or IP tracking for personal profiling.
✕ Financial or payment information.

💾 Local Data Storage & Security

All your tasks, preferences, and data remain exclusively on your device inside a local database. No cloud synchronization occurs. If you uninstall the application, all data is permanently deleted from your phone.

🤖 Gemini AI Assistant Integration

When you interact with the Gemini AI Assistant feature, only the text prompt you send is transmitted to the official Google Gemini API to return a response. Your saved tasks and personal preferences are never shared or uploaded.
