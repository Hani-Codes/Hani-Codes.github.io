Welcome to GPS Fleet ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application GPS Fleet. Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the application.

1. Information We Collect & Device Permissions

To provide accurate real-time GPS tracking, vehicle telemetry, and PDF route exports, GPS Fleet requires specific android device permissions:

📍 Location Data (Foreground & Background GPS Tracking)
We request ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION to continuously track physical vehicle positions, compute driving speed, draw trip route polylines, and trigger Geofence safety alerts even when the app is running in the background.
📡 Hardware Device Identifiers (IMEI & Serial Number)
We collect and process your Tracki / Traccar hardware IMEI serial number to authenticate vehicle pairing, establish server connections, and maintain multi-vehicle fleet profiles.
📄 Local Storage Access (PDF & CSV Telemetry Exports)
We utilize local storage permissions to allow users to generate, format, and save 30-day historical trip telemetry reports in PDF and CSV format on their device.
🔔 Safety Push Notifications (Speed Alerts & Anti-Theft Guard)
We use POST_NOTIFICATIONS to deliver urgent real-time alerts when a vehicle exceeds user-defined speed limits or leaves a designated Geofence perimeter.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following specific purposes:

  • Real-Time Fleet Visualization: Display live vehicle markers on interactive dark, light, and USGS High-Res Satellite maps.
  • Trip History & Analytics: Retrieve and render past route logs, driving speeds, and stationary durations for up to 30 days.
  • Safety & Theft Prevention: Execute Anti-Theft Guard monitoring and trigger immediate visual/auditory speed notifications.
  • Subscription & Premium Features: Process Google Play In-App Billing subscriptions to unlock unlimited fleet profiles and ad-free experience.

3. Advertising & Third-Party Services (AdMob)

Non-subscribed users may see non-intrusive banner advertisements served by Google AdMob. We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal GPS telemetry data or location history to third-party advertisers or market research companies.

4. Data Security & Retention

We implement administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your personal location data. Your telemetry logs are encrypted during transmission using TLS/SSL protocols. Data is retained only as long as necessary to provide service.

5. Data Deletion Requests & User Rights

You have the right to request deletion of your account and vehicle tracking history at any time. You can remove individual vehicles directly within the app using the Remove Device option, or submit a full data deletion request by contacting our support team.
