Sync - Huawei Watch Fit 3
Privacy Policy  |  Effective Date: August 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Sync - Huawei Watch Fit 3 application ("we", "our", or "the App") handles your health, fitness, and personal data. Your privacy is our highest priority.

🛡️ Zero Data Collection & Serverless Design

Sync - Huawei Watch Fit 3 operates entirely locally on your mobile device. We do not use external databases, we do not run remote servers, and we never collect, upload, share, or sell your personal health statistics to anyone.

1. Data Access & Permissions

To display your wearable metrics, Sync - Huawei Watch Fit 3 requests read-only permissions to access Google's secure Health Connect database. Specifically, we access:

  • Steps Count: To calculate and display your daily step progress.
  • Active Energy Burned (Calories): To show calories burned during activities.
  • Heart Rate: To calculate and present your daily average heart rate.

The App requests strictly read-only access. We never write, modify, or delete any health records in your Health Connect store.

2. Local Processing & Security

All metrics queried from Health Connect are stored locally on your device's secure storage to maintain session consistency.

  • No user health data ever leaves your device over the internet.
  • No cloud or remote servers are connected to this App.
  • All computations are performed locally on your device's processor.

3. Advertising Services Google AdMob

Sync - Huawei Watch Fit 3 displays ads provided by Google AdMob. Ad content is served by Google and governed by Google's Privacy Policy. We do not collect or access any data related to ad serving.

4. Google API Policies

All data handling adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. User Control & Data Deletion

You retain full control over your health metrics:

  • Revoke Permissions: Android Settings → Privacy → Health Connect → App permissions → Turn off.
  • Clear Data: Android Settings → Apps → Sync - Huawei Watch Fit 3 → Storage → Clear Data.
