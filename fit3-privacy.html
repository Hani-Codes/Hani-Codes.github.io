Sync - Huawei Watch Fit 3

Privacy Policy  |  Effective Date: August 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies strictly to the mobile application Sync - Huawei Watch Fit 3 . We prioritize user data protection and privacy above all else.

🛡️ Serverless & Local Processing Only

Sync - Huawei Watch Fit 3 runs 100% locally on your phone. We do not use remote servers, and we never collect, upload, or sell your health metrics.

1. Health Connect Permissions

To display companion metrics on your dashboard, the App requests read-only integration with Google Health Connect:

  • Steps Count: Displays your daily step count and goal progress.
  • Active Energy Burned (Calories): Displays active calories burned during activity.
  • Heart Rate: Calculates and presents your daily average heart rate.

All data access is strictly read-only. The App never modifies or deletes your Health Connect records.

2. Storage & Security

Metrics pulled from Health Connect are cached locally on your device to maintain dashboard performance between sessions. No health data ever leaves your device.

3. Advertisements (Google AdMob)

This App uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob operates under Google's Privacy Policy. No personal health metrics are shared with AdMob or used for advertising.

4. Google API Policy Compliance

All data handling strictly complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements.

5. Managing Your Data

You have full control over your privacy permissions:

  • Revoke Permissions: Android Settings → Privacy → Health Connect → App permissions → Sync - Huawei Watch Fit 3.
  • Delete Stored Data: Android Settings → Apps → Sync - Huawei Watch Fit 3 → Storage → Clear Data.
