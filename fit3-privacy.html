Sync - Huawei Watch Fit 3

Official Privacy Policy  |  Updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy governs the data usage and privacy standards for the mobile application Sync - Huawei Watch Fit 3 ("the App"). We are committed to safeguarding your personal information.

🛡️ Zero Remote Server Data Collection

Sync - Huawei Watch Fit 3 operates exclusively on a local, on-device architecture. We do not use remote servers, and we never collect, upload, share, or sell your health metrics.

1. Health Connect Data Access

To display fitness metrics on your dashboard, the App requests read-only integration with Google's Health Connect database:

  • Steps Count: Used to display your daily step total and progress goal.
  • Active Energy Burned (Calories): Used to calculate active calories burned.
  • Heart Rate: Used to present your daily average heart rate.

All requested permissions are strictly read-only. The App does not write or modify your Health Connect records.

2. Storage & Data Security

Metrics fetched from Health Connect are temporarily cached on your local device storage to ensure smooth app performance. No health data ever leaves your phone or travels over the internet.

3. Advertisements (Google AdMob)

This App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob operations are governed by Google's Privacy Policy. No personal health metrics are shared with AdMob or used for ad targeting.

4. Google API Policy Compliance

All data handling strictly complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements.

5. Managing Your Privacy & Data

You retain full authority over your data rights:

  • Revoke Permissions: Android Settings → Privacy → Health Connect → App permissions → Sync - Huawei Watch Fit 3.
  • Delete Stored Data: Android Settings → Apps → Sync - Huawei Watch Fit 3 → Storage → Clear Data.
