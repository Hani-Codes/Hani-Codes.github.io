Sync - Huawei Watch Fit 3
Privacy Policy  |  Effective Date: August 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Sync - Huawei Watch Fit 3 ("the App"). Your privacy is our priority, and we are committed to keeping all your data secure.

🛡️ Zero Data Collection Commitment

Sync - Huawei Watch Fit 3 operates entirely locally on your device. We do not use remote servers, and we never collect, upload, share, or sell your health data.

1. Data Access & Permissions

To display your wearable metrics, the App requests read-only permissions to access Google's secure Health Connect database:

  • Steps Count: To calculate and display daily step progress.
  • Active Energy Burned (Calories): To show calories burned during activities.
  • Heart Rate: To present your daily average heart rate.

Access is strictly read-only. We never write, modify, or delete any health records in Health Connect.

2. Local Processing & Security

All metrics read from Health Connect are stored locally on your device's secure storage to maintain session consistency. No health data ever leaves your phone over the internet.

3. Advertising Google AdMob

This App uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob operates under Google's Privacy Policy. No health data is ever shared with AdMob or used for advertising.

4. Google API Policies

All data handling adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. User Rights & Data Deletion

You have full control over your data:

  • Revoke Access: Android Settings → Privacy → Health Connect → App permissions → Turn off.
  • Clear Data: Android Settings → Apps → Sync - Huawei Watch Fit 3 → Storage → Clear Data.
