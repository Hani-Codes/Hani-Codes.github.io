Syns - Huawei Watch GT 4
Privacy Policy  |  Effective Date: August 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the "Syns - Huawei Watch GT 4" application ("we", "our", or "the App") handles your health, fitness, and personal data. Your privacy is our highest priority.

🛡️ Zero Data Collection Commitment

This application operates entirely locally on your device. We do not use external databases, we do not run remote servers, and we never collect, upload, share, or sell your personal health data to anyone.

1. Data Access & Permissions

To display your wearable dashboard metrics, the App requests read-only permissions to access Google's secure Health Connect database. Specifically, we access:

  • Steps Count: To calculate and display your daily step progress.
  • Active Energy Burned (Calories): To show the calories burned during activities.
  • Heart Rate: To calculate and present your daily average heart rate.

The App only requests read-only access. We never write, modify, or delete any health records in your Health Connect store.

2. Local Processing & Security

All information read from Health Connect is processed in-memory and stored locally on your phone's secure storage to retain your metrics between app sessions.

  • No cloud or remote servers are used by this App.
  • Your health data is never transmitted over the internet.
  • All computations are performed locally on your device's processor.

3. Advertising AdMob

This App displays ads provided by Google AdMob. Ad content is served by Google and governed by Google's Privacy Policy. We do not collect or access any data related to ad serving.

4. Third-Party Services & Google Policies

Our App integrates with Google Health Connect. All data handling adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. User Rights & Data Deletion

You have full ownership and control over your data:

  • Revoke Health Access: Go to Android Settings → Privacy → Health Connect → App permissions → Remove all.
  • Delete Cached Data: Go to Android Settings → Apps → Syns - Huawei Watch GT 4 → Storage → Clear Data.
