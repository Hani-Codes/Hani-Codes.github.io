Smartwatch Sync Companion
Privacy Policy | Effective Date: August 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the **Smartwatch Sync Companion** application ("we", "our", or "the App") handles your health, fitness, and personal data. Your privacy is our highest priority, and we are committed to keeping all your metrics safe and secure.

🛡️ Zero Data Collection Commitment

This application operates entirely locally on your device. We do not use external databases, we do not run remote servers, and we never collect, upload, share, or sell your health statistics to anyone.

1. Data Access & Permissions

To display your wearable dashboard metrics, the App requests read-only integration permissions to access Google's secure Health Connect database on your mobile phone. Specifically, we access:

  • Steps count: To calculate and display your daily steps progress.
  • Active Energy Burned (Calories): To show the active calories burned during activities and exercises.
  • Heart Rate: To determine and present your daily average heart rate.

The App only requests read-only access. We never write, modify, or delete any health records in your Health Connect store.

2. Local Processing & Security

All information read from Health Connect is processed in-memory and saved locally on your phone's secure storage (SharedPreferences) to retain your metrics between app launches.

  • No cloud or remote servers are linked to this App.
  • Your data is never transmitted over the internet.
  • All computations (e.g., average heart rate, progress ratios) are performed locally on the device's processor.

3. Third-Party Services & Google Policies

Our App integrates with Google Health Connect. The transfer of information received from Health Connect to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Since we do not share or upload your data, we strictly fulfill and exceed all privacy requirements.

4. User Rights & Data Deletion

You have full ownership and control over your health metrics:

  • Revoke Access: You can revoke the App's access to Health Connect at any time via Android System Settings -> Privacy -> Health Connect.
  • Delete Cached Data: You can completely erase all local statistics stored by this App by clearing the app cache (Android Settings -> Apps -> Companion App -> Storage -> Clear Data).
