Sync – Huawei Watch Fit 3
Privacy Policy  |  Effective Date: August 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Sync – Huawei Watch Fit 3 application ("we", "our", or "the App") handles your health, fitness, and personal data. Your privacy is our highest priority, and we are committed to keeping all your metrics safe and secure.

🛡️ Zero Data Collection Commitment

This application operates entirely locally on your device. We do not use external databases, we do not run remote servers, and we never collect, upload, share, or sell your personal health data to anyone.

1. Data Access & Permissions

To display your wearable dashboard metrics, the App requests read-only permissions to access Google's secure Health Connect database. Specifically, we access:

  • Steps Count: To calculate and display your daily step progress.
  • Active Energy Burned (Calories): To show the calories burned during activities and exercises.
  • Heart Rate: To calculate and present your daily average heart rate.

The App only requests read-only access. We never write, modify, or delete any health records in your Health Connect store.

2. Local Processing & Security

All information read from Health Connect is processed in-memory and stored locally on your phone's secure storage (SharedPreferences) to retain your metrics between app sessions.

  • No cloud or remote servers are used by this App.
  • Your health data is never transmitted over the internet.
  • All computations (e.g., average heart rate, step progress) are performed locally on your device's processor.

3. Advertising AdMob

This App uses Google AdMob to display banner advertisements. AdMob may collect certain non-personal device information to serve relevant ads, including:

  • Device identifiers (Advertising ID)
  • IP address and general location (country/region level)
  • Device type, OS version, and language settings
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

This data is collected and processed by Google LLC in accordance with their own privacy policy. We do not have access to this data. You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time via:

  • Android Settings → Google → Ads → "Delete advertising ID" or opt out of Ads Personalization.

For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy and Google AdMob's Data Usage Policy.

4. Third-Party Services & Google Policies

Our App integrates with Google Health Connect. The transfer of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Since we do not upload or share your health data, we fully comply with all applicable requirements.

5. User Rights & Data Deletion

You have full ownership and control over your data:

  • Revoke Health Access: Go to Android Settings → Privacy → Health Connect → App permissions → Sync → Remove all.
  • Delete Cached Data: Go to Android Settings → Apps → Sync → Storage → Clear Data. This immediately removes all locally stored metrics from your device.
  • Opt out of Ad Personalization: Go to Android Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID.
