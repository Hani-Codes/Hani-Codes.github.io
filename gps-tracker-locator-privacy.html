Welcome to GPS Tracker & Locator ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our iOS application GPS Tracker & Locator. By using the app you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the application.

1. Information We Collect & iOS Permissions

To provide live GPS tracking, trip history and report exports, the app uses the following, and only when needed:

📍 Location — While Using the App
With your permission (NSLocationWhenInUseUsageDescription) we access your location only while the app is open, to show your own position on the live map next to your GPS trackers. We do not track your location in the background.
📡 Your GPS Tracker's IMEI / Serial
You manually enter the IMEI or serial number printed on your GPS tracker to pair it to your account. This is the identifier of your separate tracking hardware — not your iPhone. iOS does not allow apps to read your phone's IMEI, and we never do.
🔐 Automatic Secure Account
To store your paired trackers and their trip history, the app creates a private account for you on our tracking server automatically and silently. You are never asked for a password, and no server address or credentials are shown to you.
📄 On-Device Exports (PDF & CSV)
Trip reports are generated on your device and shared through the standard iOS Share Sheet. No photo-library or file-system permission is required.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Live Map: Show your tracker's position on interactive Apple Maps (standard and satellite).
  • Trip History: Retrieve and display past routes, speeds and durations for up to 30 days.
  • Safety: Geofence radius and speed-alert features you configure.
  • Premium: Process subscriptions to unlock unlimited devices, full history and an ad-free experience.

3. Advertising & Apple App Tracking Transparency

Non-subscribed users may see banner advertisements served by Google AdMob, which may use advertising identifiers. In line with Apple's App Tracking Transparency, you control whether apps may track you in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. We never sell, trade, or rent your GPS or location data. Subscribing to Premium removes all ads.

4. Subscriptions & Payments

Premium is sold through Apple In-App Purchase (StoreKit). Payment is charged to your Apple ID. You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime in App Store › Account › Subscriptions. We never see or store your card or payment details — these are handled entirely by Apple.

5. Data Security & Retention

Location and telemetry data are transmitted over encrypted TLS/SSL connections. Your account credentials are stored securely in the device Keychain. We retain data only as long as necessary to provide the service.

6. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You can remove individual trackers in the app using Unlink / Reset Device. To request full deletion of your account and tracking history, email us at the address below. Subscriptions are cancelled through the App Store.

7. Children's Privacy

GPS Tracker & Locator is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

1. Erfasste Informationen & iOS-Berechtigungen

Für Live-Ortung, Fahrtenverlauf und Berichtsexporte nutzt die App Folgendes – und nur bei Bedarf:

📍 Standort — während der App-Nutzung
Mit Ihrer Erlaubnis (NSLocationWhenInUseUsageDescription) greifen wir nur bei geöffneter App auf Ihren Standort zu, um Ihre Position auf der Live-Karte neben Ihren Trackern anzuzeigen. Wir verfolgen Ihren Standort nicht im Hintergrund.

Fahrtberichte werden auf Ihrem Gerät erstellt und über das Standard-iOS-Teilen-Menü geteilt. Es ist keine Foto- oder Dateisystem-Berechtigung erforderlich.
