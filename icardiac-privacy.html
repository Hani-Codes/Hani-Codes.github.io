Privacy & Data Protection Policy

Your privacy and health data security are our highest priorities. iCardiac is engineered with a strict on-device local architecture ensuring your personal health metrics never leave your smartphone.

Application: iCardiac • Last Updated: September 2026 • Version 1.0
🔒

1. 100% On-Device Local Storage Architecture

We operate zero remote cloud databases collecting, hosting, or indexing your confidential health metrics:

  • Sandboxed Local Storage: All pulse measurements, blood pressure logs, blood oxygen (SpO2) readings, blood sugar data, and medication schedules reside exclusively within your device's isolated application storage.
  • No Sign-Up or Accounts: You are never asked to create an account, register an email address, or provide personal credentials. The application functions entirely anonymously.
  • Complete Data Sovereignty: No external entity or remote server can access, read, or monetize your health logs.
📸

2. Camera & Optical Sensor Privacy (Photoplethysmography)

To compute your heart rate and heart rate variability (HRV), iCardiac accesses your smartphone's camera and LED flashlight using standard optical photoplethysmography (PPG) algorithms:

⚠️ Strict Zero-Recording Guarantee: The application NEVER captures, saves, encodes, or transmits photographs or video recordings of your fingertip, face, or environment. Video frame analysis takes place entirely in volatile device memory (RAM) in real time and is discarded immediately after extracting numerical pulse values.
👤

3. User Profile Metrics & Technical Diagnostics

  • User-Entered Metrics: User-provided parameters (such as age, gender, height, and weight) are used strictly on-device to compute Body Mass Index (BMI) and biological heart age estimates based on published physiological benchmarks.
  • Limited Technical Diagnostics: Non-personally identifiable technical diagnostics (such as device model, Android OS version, and language locale) may be processed via standard Android OS APIs solely to ensure user interface responsiveness and crash resolution.
🌐

4. Third-Party Integrations & Advertising

We strictly prohibit selling, renting, or leasing your data to data brokers or third parties. External services are limited to the following verified providers:

  • Google AdMob: Serves standard mobile advertisements for free-tier users in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and privacy regulations. Subscribers to iCardiac Pro enjoy an ad-free experience.
  • Secure Cloud AI Technologies: Powers real-time interactive wellbeing responses and educational lifestyle guidance anonymously without retaining personal profiles or associating requests with your identity.
🗑️

5. User Data Erasure & Complete Control

You retain absolute control over your health information at all times:

  • You can review, modify, or delete individual pulse, blood pressure, or blood sugar logs directly from the application records.
  • You can update your age, gender, height, and weight metrics anytime in the "My Profile" screen.
  • Permanent Total Erasure: Because all data is stored on-device, clearing application storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the app permanently deletes all stored records instantly.
⚕️

6. Medical Disclaimer & Children's Privacy

  • Non-Clinical Wellness Tool: iCardiac is designed solely for personal fitness, lifestyle guidance, and wellness tracking. It is NOT an FDA-cleared or CE-certified medical diagnostic device and cannot diagnose, treat, prevent, or cure any medical condition or disease. Always seek professional advice from a certified physician or cardiologist for health concerns.
  • Children's Privacy: iCardiac is intended for adults aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect or solicit data from children under the age of 13.
